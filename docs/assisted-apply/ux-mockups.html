"How this works" explainer (Dialog, same as ConfirmDialog shell)

How Assisted apply works

Opportun cannot apply to a job for you, and does not try to. You do the applying, Opportun keeps the record.

  1. You choose a resume. Opportun saves an exact copy so the record shows what you used.
  2. You review. You check the resume, your details and your answers on one screen.
  3. You open the real form. It opens in a new tab on the employer's own application site.
  4. You press that site's Submit button. Only you can do this.
  5. You tell Opportun. It then adds the application to your timeline and watches for a confirmation email.

Opportun never sends anything to the application site, never logs in there, and never applies to more than one job at a time.

2. Submission status chips

New SubmissionStatusChip wraps the existing Badge variants (components/ui/badge.tsx). Every status has a distinct icon and its own text; colour is only a third cue. The chip carries a visually hidden prefix "Submission status:".

  • Submission status: DraftYou are preparing it. Nothing is final.
  • Submission status: ReviewedYou checked it. The real form is not open yet.
  • Submission status: Opened on ATSThe form was opened. Opportun does not know if you submitted.
  • Submission status: SubmittedYou told Opportun you pressed Submit.
  • Submission status: ConfirmedA confirmation email or page backs it up.
  • Submission status: No confirmation seenNot a failure. Many employers send nothing.
  • Submission status: ATS refusedThe application site rejected the form.
  • Submission status: FailedIt could not be completed. Nothing was submitted.
  • Submission status: AbandonedYou stopped before submitting.

Forced-colours / greyscale check: the nine chips differ by icon shape and label, and Abandoned also differs by dashed border.
